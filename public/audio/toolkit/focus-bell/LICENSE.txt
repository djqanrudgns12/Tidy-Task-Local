Runtime loudness revision 4
All four files are measured with mono ITU-R BS.1770 K-weighting and gating, then normalized to
-12 LUFS integrated. A -1.5 dBFS peak ceiling is used for compressed files. Bell and siren need
only linear attenuation; bomb and fart use soft tanh compression so their average loudness can
rise without digital clipping. Exact before/after values: output/qa/focus-bell-loudness-normalization.json.
